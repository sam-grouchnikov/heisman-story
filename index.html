The Coach Behind the Trophy

John Heisman

Every year, the Heisman Trophy goes to an outstanding college football player. It took John Heisman’s name after his death in 1936, honoring a coach who had already changed how the game was played.[6][27]

Heisman coached at Georgia Tech from 1904 to 1919. He pressed for the rule change that legalized the forward pass in 1906 and led Georgia Tech to its first national championship in 1917.[5][6]

The story begins in the fall of 1869, when Heisman was born in Cleveland, Ohio.[5]
